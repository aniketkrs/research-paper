# Harvard Citation Style

## In-Text Citations

### Single author
- (Smith, 2023)
- Smith (2023) argues that...

### Two authors
- (Smith and Jones, 2023)
- Smith and Jones (2023) found that...

### Three or more authors
- (Smith et al., 2023)
- Smith et al. (2023) demonstrated...

### Multiple citations
- (Smith, 2023; Jones, 2022; Brown, 2021) — alphabetical order
- Several studies support this finding (Brown, 2021; Jones, 2022; Smith, 2023)

### Direct quote
- (Smith, 2023, p. 45)
- Smith (2023, p. 45) stated that "exact words"

### Secondary citation
- (Smith, 2020, cited in Jones, 2023)

### Organization as author
- (World Health Organization, 2023)
- First mention: (World Health Organization [WHO], 2023)
- Subsequent: (WHO, 2023)

### No date
- (Smith, n.d.)

### Multiple works by same author, same year
- (Smith, 2023a)
- (Smith, 2023b)

## Reference List Format

### Journal Article
Smith, J.A. and Jones, B.C. (2023) 'Title of article in sentence case', *Journal Name in Title Case*, 45(2), pp. 123-145. doi:10.1000/xyz123.

### Book
Smith, J.A. (2023) *Title of Book in Italics*. 3rd edn. London: Publisher Name.

### Book Chapter
Smith, J.A. (2023) 'Chapter title', in Jones, B.C. (ed.) *Book Title*. London: Publisher, pp. 50-75.

### Conference Paper
Smith, J.A. (2023) 'Paper title', *Proceedings of Conference Name*, City, Date. London: Publisher, pp. 1-10.

### Website
Smith, J.A. (2023) *Title of web page*. Available at: https://www.example.com (Accessed: 15 January 2024).

### Report
Organization Name (2023) *Report Title*. Report no. 123. City: Publisher.

### Thesis
Smith, J.A. (2023) *Title of thesis*. PhD thesis. University Name.

### Preprint
Smith, J.A. (2023) 'Title of paper', *arXiv preprint*, arXiv:2301.12345.

### Dataset
Smith, J.A. (2023) *Dataset Name* [Dataset]. Available at: https://doi.org/10.xxx (Accessed: 15 January 2024).

---

## Reference List Rules

- Alphabetical by first author's surname
- Hanging indent (second line onwards indented)
- Use "and" between last two authors (not "&")
- If 4+ authors: list all in reference list (et al. only in-text)
- Use sentence case for article titles
- Use italics for journal names, book titles
- Include DOI where available
- Include "Available at" and "Accessed" date for online sources
- No "Retrieved from" — use "Available at"
