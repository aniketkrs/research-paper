# MLA and Chicago Citation Styles

---

## MLA (9th Edition)

### In-Text Citations
- Parenthetical: (Author Page)
- Narrative: Author states "quote" (Page).
- No year in in-text citation (MLA uses author-page, not author-year)
- No comma between author and page

### Examples
- One author: (Smith 45)
- Two authors: (Smith and Jones 78)
- Three+ authors: (Smith et al. 102)
- No author: ("Title" 12)
- Multiple works: (Smith 45; Jones 78)
- Indirect source: (qtd. in Jones 33)

### Works Cited Format

**Journal article:**
Smith, John A., and Jane B. Jones. "Title of Article." *Journal Name*, vol. 45, no. 2, 2023, pp. 123-45.

**Book:**
Smith, John A. *Title of Book*. Publisher, 2023.

**Chapter in edited book:**
Smith, John A. "Chapter Title." *Book Title*, edited by Jane Jones, Publisher, 2023, pp. 50-75.

**Website:**
Smith, John. "Title of Page." *Website Name*, Publisher, Day Month Year, URL.

**Conference paper:**
Smith, John. "Paper Title." *Conference Name*, City, Year.

### MLA Rules
- Works Cited alphabetical by first author surname
- Hanging indent
- Italicize titles of standalone works (books, journals, websites)
- Quotation marks for titles of parts (articles, chapters, pages)
- No "p." or "pp." for page numbers
- Use et al. for 3+ authors
- Include URL or DOI (no "Retrieved from")
- Access date only if no publication date

## Chicago Manual of Style (17th Edition)

### Two Systems

**Notes-Bibliography (NB) — Humanities:**
- Footnotes or endnotes for citations
- Bibliography at end
- Used in: history, literature, arts

**Author-Date (AD) — Sciences:**
- Parenthetical citations (Author Year)
- Reference list at end
- Used in: social sciences, natural sciences

---

### Notes-Bibliography System

**First footnote (full):**
1. John A. Smith and Jane B. Jones, "Title of Article," *Journal Name* 45, no. 2 (2023): 125.

**Subsequent footnotes (short):**
2. Smith and Jones, "Title of Article," 130.

**Ibid (immediately following same source):**
3. Ibid., 132.

**Bibliography entry:**
Smith, John A., and Jane B. Jones. "Title of Article." *Journal Name* 45, no. 2 (2023): 123–45.

### Notes-Bibliography Formats

**Book:**
- Note: John A. Smith, *Title of Book* (City: Publisher, 2023), 45.
- Bib: Smith, John A. *Title of Book*. City: Publisher, 2023.

**Journal article:**
- Note: John A. Smith, "Article Title," *Journal* 45, no. 2 (2023): 125.
- Bib: Smith, John A. "Article Title." *Journal* 45, no. 2 (2023): 123–45.

**Chapter:**
- Note: John A. Smith, "Chapter Title," in *Book Title*, ed. Jane Jones (City: Publisher, 2023), 55.
- Bib: Smith, John A. "Chapter Title." In *Book Title*, edited by Jane Jones, 50–75. City: Publisher, 2023.

**Website:**
- Note: John Smith, "Page Title," Website Name, last modified January 15, 2023, URL.
- Bib: Smith, John. "Page Title." Website Name. Last modified January 15, 2023. URL.

---

### Author-Date System

**In-text citations:**
- (Smith 2023)
- (Smith 2023, 45) — with page
- (Smith and Jones 2023)
- (Smith et al. 2023)
- Smith (2023) argues...
- (Smith 2023; Jones 2022) — multiple

**Reference list formats:**

**Journal article:**
Smith, John A., and Jane B. Jones. 2023. "Title of Article." *Journal Name* 45 (2): 123–45. https://doi.org/xxx.

**Book:**
Smith, John A. 2023. *Title of Book*. City: Publisher.

**Chapter:**
Smith, John A. 2023. "Chapter Title." In *Book Title*, edited by Jane Jones, 50–75. City: Publisher.

**Website:**
Smith, John. 2023. "Page Title." Website Name. January 15, 2023. URL.

### Chicago Rules
- Bibliography/reference list: alphabetical by surname
- Hanging indent
- Notes-Bibliography: given name first in notes, surname first in bibliography
- Author-Date: surname first in reference list
- En-dash (–) for page ranges
- Serial comma (Oxford comma) used
- Ibid. acceptable in NB system but declining in favor of short form
- "Accessed [date]" for undated web content
