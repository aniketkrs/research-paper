# Harvard-Style Paper Template

Use for social science, business, education, humanities, and policy-adjacent
academic work. Argument-driven, theory-grounded, with **Harvard
(author, year)** citations.

> Replace every `<<...>>`.

## Skill-specific instructions for this template

1. **Citation style:** Harvard (author, year), alphabetical reference list.
2. The **theoretical framework** (§2) is conventional in social-science
   papers and often the missing piece in AI-generated drafts. Always include
   it.
3. **Hypotheses** stated formally in §4 (H1, H2 …) and tested in §6.
4. Reliability (Cronbach's α / CR / AVE) reported for every constructed
   scale.
5. **Common-method bias** addressed (Harman's single-factor / marker
   variable) for self-report surveys.
6. **Researcher positionality** stated for qualitative or mixed-methods.
7. **Implications split** into theoretical, practical, and policy.
8. Reference list is alphabetical, with hanging indent.
