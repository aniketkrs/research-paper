## Skill-specific instructions for this template

1. Citation style: **Nature numeric superscript** in the text
   (`…earlier work¹⁻³`), references in order of appearance.
2. The main text has **no "Introduction" header** — open with a context
   paragraph.
3. Word count: ~3,000 words main text + ~50 references for an Article;
   ~1,500 / ~30 for a Letter.
4. Figures: caption begins with "**Fig. N |**" (a vertical bar after the
   number).
5. Use abbreviations consistent with Nature style: "Fig." not "Figure";
   "ref. <n>" not "[n]".
6. The CRediT author-contributions statement is mandatory.
7. Data and Code availability statements are mandatory and venue-checked.
8. Extended Data is the in-line supplementary; Supplementary Information is
   a separate file.
9. References use **bold volume numbers** and a specific Nature initials-
   first author format (see `references/citation-styles.md §6`).
