# Publication Readiness Quality Rubric

## Purpose
Score the overall quality of a generated paper and determine if it meets publication standards.

---

## Scoring Dimensions (1-5 each, total /50)

### 1. Research Contribution (Weight: High)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Novel contribution clearly articulated; advances the field meaningfully |
| 4 | Solid contribution; adds value to existing knowledge |
| 3 | Incremental contribution; synthesizes but doesn't advance significantly |
| 2 | Contribution unclear; mostly restates known information |
| 1 | No discernible contribution; purely descriptive without insight |

### 2. Literature Engagement (Weight: High)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Comprehensive, current, critically synthesized; identifies clear gap |
| 4 | Good coverage; mostly synthesized; gap identified |
| 3 | Adequate sources; some synthesis; gap could be clearer |
| 2 | Sparse or outdated sources; mostly summary, little synthesis |
| 1 | Minimal engagement with literature; no gap identified |

### 3. Methodological Rigor (Weight: High)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Exemplary design; fully reproducible; all assumptions checked and reported |
| 4 | Strong design; mostly reproducible; major assumptions addressed |
| 3 | Adequate design; some gaps in reporting; basic assumptions noted |
| 2 | Weak design; methodology-question misalignment; poor reporting |
| 1 | Inappropriate methodology; not reproducible; no validation |

### 4. Evidence Quality (Weight: High)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Strong evidence base; claims fully supported; effect sizes + CI reported |
| 4 | Good evidence; most claims supported; key statistics reported |
| 3 | Adequate evidence; some unsupported claims; basic statistics present |
| 2 | Weak evidence; many unsupported claims; incomplete statistical reporting |
| 1 | Claims unsupported; speculation presented as finding |

### 5. Argument Coherence (Weight: Medium)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Flawless logical flow; each section builds on previous; clear narrative arc |
| 4 | Strong flow; minor gaps; overall coherent argument |
| 3 | Generally coherent; some logical jumps; mostly connected |
| 2 | Disjointed sections; weak transitions; argument hard to follow |
| 1 | No coherent argument; sections seem unrelated |

### 6. Writing Quality (Weight: Medium)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Elegant academic prose; clear, precise, engaging; human-sounding |
| 4 | Good academic writing; clear and professional; minor issues |
| 3 | Adequate writing; some awkward phrasing; generally clear |
| 2 | Poor academic tone; AI-sounding; vague or repetitive |
| 1 | Unprofessional; incoherent; inappropriate tone throughout |

### 7. Citation Quality (Weight: Medium)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Perfect formatting; high-quality sources; comprehensive coverage; no orphans |
| 4 | Consistent formatting; good sources; adequate coverage |
| 3 | Minor formatting issues; acceptable sources; some gaps |
| 2 | Inconsistent formatting; weak sources; significant gaps |
| 1 | Major formatting errors; poor sources; many uncited claims |

### 8. Visualization Quality (Weight: Medium)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | All visualizations add insight; properly captioned; well-integrated with text |
| 4 | Most visualizations effective; good captions; referenced in text |
| 3 | Adequate visualizations; some captions incomplete; mostly referenced |
| 2 | Visualizations unclear or inappropriate; poor integration |
| 1 | Missing or irrelevant visualizations; no captions; not referenced |

### 9. Formatting Compliance (Weight: Low)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Perfect compliance with chosen format; all sections present; correct structure |
| 4 | Minor deviations; all required sections present |
| 3 | Some structural issues; most sections present |
| 2 | Significant formatting problems; missing sections |
| 1 | Does not follow any recognizable academic format |

### 10. Practical Value (Weight: Low)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Clear implications for theory AND practice; actionable insights; high relevance |
| 4 | Good implications; some actionable insights |
| 3 | Some implications mentioned; generic recommendations |
| 2 | Implications vague or disconnected from findings |
| 1 | No practical value; purely academic exercise with no applicability |

## Overall Assessment

### Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Assessment | Action |
|-------------|-----------|--------|
| 45-50 | Publication ready | Submit with confidence |
| 40-44 | Near ready | Minor revisions (1-2 areas) |
| 35-39 | Promising | Moderate revisions (3-4 areas) |
| 28-34 | Needs work | Major revisions required |
| 20-27 | Significant issues | Substantial rework needed |
| <20 | Not viable | Restart with revised approach |

### Minimum Thresholds (any score below these = not ready)

- Research Contribution: ≥ 3
- Methodological Rigor: ≥ 3
- Evidence Quality: ≥ 3
- Writing Quality: ≥ 3

## Publication Readiness Checklist

```
MANDATORY (paper cannot proceed without these):
□ Title is specific, informative, and concise
□ Abstract summarizes all key elements (150-300 words)
□ Research questions/objectives clearly stated
□ Methodology described with sufficient detail for replication
□ All major claims have supporting evidence/citations
□ Results directly address research questions
□ Limitations honestly acknowledged
□ Reference list complete and properly formatted
□ All figures/tables captioned and referenced in text
□ Consistent formatting throughout

STRONGLY RECOMMENDED:
□ Effect sizes reported alongside significance tests
□ Confidence intervals for key estimates
□ Simple explanation boxes for complex concepts
□ At least one process/architecture diagram
□ Future research directions are specific (not generic)
□ Implications stated for both theory and practice
□ Anti-AI-voice filter applied (no robotic phrasing)
□ Word count within target range for paper type

DESIRABLE:
□ Compelling opening hook (not "In recent years")
□ Visual storytelling (data visualization narrative)
□ Cross-references between sections create cohesion
□ Acknowledgment of alternative explanations
□ Connection to broader societal relevance
□ Reproducibility information (code/data availability)
```

---

## Quick Assessment (When Full Rubric is Excessive)

For rapid quality check, answer these five questions:

1. **Would I cite this?** — Does it contribute something citable to the field?
2. **Could I replicate it?** — Is the methodology sufficiently described?
3. **Do the conclusions follow?** — Are claims supported by the presented evidence?
4. **Is it readable?** — Can an informed reader follow the argument smoothly?
5. **Is it honest?** — Are limitations acknowledged and claims appropriately scoped?

If all five = yes → paper is likely publication-ready with minor polishing.
If any = no → that dimension needs revision before the paper is complete.
