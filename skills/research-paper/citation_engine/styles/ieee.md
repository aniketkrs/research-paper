# IEEE Citation Style

## In-Text Citations

### Numbered references (order of appearance)
- [1]
- [2], [3]
- [1]–[3] (consecutive range)
- [1], [4], [7] (non-consecutive)

### Placement
- At end of clause: "...as demonstrated previously [5]."
- After author name: "Smith [5] proposed..."
- Multiple: "...confirmed by several studies [2], [5]–[7]."

### Rules
- Numbers assigned in ORDER OF FIRST APPEARANCE (not alphabetical)
- Same source always uses same number throughout paper
- Use brackets [ ], not parentheses
- Number is part of the sentence grammatically: "In [4], the authors..."

## Reference List Format

### Journal Article
[N] A. B. Author, C. D. Author, and E. F. Author, "Title of article in sentence case," *Abbrev. J. Name*, vol. 45, no. 2, pp. 123–145, Mon. Year, doi: 10.1000/xyz123.

### Journal Article (6+ authors)
[N] A. B. Author et al., "Title of article," *Abbrev. J. Name*, vol. 45, no. 2, pp. 123–145, Feb. 2023.

### Conference Paper
[N] A. B. Author and C. D. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. Full Conf. Name (ACRONYM)*, City, Country, Year, pp. 1–6.

### Book
[N] A. B. Author, *Title of Book in Title Case*, Xth ed. City, State/Country: Publisher, Year.

### Book Chapter
[N] A. B. Author, "Title of chapter," in *Title of Book*, A. B. Editor, Ed. City: Publisher, Year, pp. 1–50.

### Technical Report
[N] A. B. Author, "Title of report," Dept., Univ./Company, City, State, Tech. Rep. RPT-2023-001, Year.

### Thesis
[N] A. B. Author, "Title of thesis," Ph.D. dissertation, Dept., Univ., City, State, Year.

### Standard
[N] *Title of Standard*, Standard IEEE XXX-YYYY, Year.

### Patent
[N] A. B. Author, "Title of patent," U.S. Patent X XXX XXX, Mon. Day, Year.

### Website/Online
[N] A. B. Author. "Title of page." Organization. Accessed: Mon. Day, Year. [Online]. Available: https://url

### Preprint
[N] A. B. Author and C. D. Author, "Title," arXiv:2301.12345 [cs.XX], Year.

### Dataset
[N] A. B. Author, "Title of dataset," Year. [Online]. Available: https://url

## IEEE Formatting Rules

- Abbreviate journal names (IEEE standard abbreviations)
- Abbreviate months: Jan., Feb., Mar., Apr., May, Jun., Jul., Aug., Sep., Oct., Nov., Dec.
- Author names: initials before surname (A. B. Smith)
- Use "and" before last author (not "&")
- 6+ authors: first author et al.
- Titles in quotes, journal/book names in italics
- No bold in reference list
- Number references in order of appearance
- "pp." for page ranges, "p." for single page
- State abbreviations for US locations (e.g., "New York, NY, USA")
