# APA 7th Edition Citation Style

## In-Text Citations

### Single author
- (Smith, 2023)
- Smith (2023) found that...

### Two authors
- (Smith & Jones, 2023) — use ampersand in parenthetical
- Smith and Jones (2023) — use "and" in narrative

### Three or more authors
- (Smith et al., 2023) — always use et al. from first citation
- Smith et al. (2023) demonstrated...

### Multiple citations
- (Brown, 2021; Jones, 2022; Smith, 2023) — alphabetical, semicolons

### Direct quote (under 40 words)
- (Smith, 2023, p. 45)
- Smith (2023) stated that "exact quote" (p. 45).

### Direct quote (40+ words) — block quote
- Indented, no quotation marks, citation after final period

### Organization as author
- First: (American Psychological Association [APA], 2023)
- Subsequent: (APA, 2023)

### Multiple works, same author, same year
- (Smith, 2023a, 2023b)

### No date
- (Smith, n.d.)

### Personal communication (not in reference list)
- (J. Smith, personal communication, March 15, 2023)

## Reference List Format (APA 7th)

### Journal Article
Smith, J. A., & Jones, B. C. (2023). Title of article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case*, *45*(2), 123–145. https://doi.org/10.1000/xyz123

### Journal Article (21+ authors)
List first 19 authors... Author, A. A., Author, B. B., Author, C. C. [list 19] ... Author, Last. (Year). Title. *Journal*, *vol*(issue), pages. DOI

### Book
Smith, J. A. (2023). *Title of book in sentence case* (3rd ed.). Publisher Name.

### Edited Book Chapter
Smith, J. A. (2023). Chapter title in sentence case. In B. C. Jones (Ed.), *Book title* (pp. 50–75). Publisher.

### Conference Paper (published proceedings)
Smith, J. A. (2023). Paper title. In B. C. Jones (Ed.), *Proceedings of the Conference Name* (pp. 1–10). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxx

### Conference Presentation (unpublished)
Smith, J. A. (2023, March 15–17). *Title of presentation* [Conference presentation]. Conference Name, City, Country.

### Website
Smith, J. A. (2023, January 15). *Title of page*. Site Name. https://www.example.com/path

### Report (with DOI or URL)
Organization. (2023). *Title of report* (Report No. 123). https://www.example.com/report

### Dissertation/Thesis
Smith, J. A. (2023). *Title of dissertation* [Doctoral dissertation, University Name]. Database Name. https://url

### Preprint
Smith, J. A. (2023). Title of paper. *arXiv*. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.12345

### Dataset
Smith, J. A. (2023). *Title of dataset* [Data set]. Repository Name. https://doi.org/xxx

### Software
Smith, J. A. (2023). *Software Name* (Version 2.0) [Computer software]. https://url

## APA 7th Specific Rules

- Use en-dash (–) for page ranges, not hyphen
- Use https://doi.org/ format for DOIs (not "doi:" prefix)
- No "Retrieved from" for content with DOIs
- Include "Retrieved [date] from [URL]" only for content that may change
- Italicize journal name AND volume number
- Up to 20 authors: list all; 21+: first 19 ... last author
- Use "&" before last author in reference list
- Use "and" in narrative citations, "&" in parenthetical
- Double-space reference list
- Hanging indent (0.5 inch)
- Alphabetical by first author surname
- Same author, multiple years: oldest first
- Same author, same year: add a, b, c
